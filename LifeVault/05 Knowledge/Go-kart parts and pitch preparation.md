## Everything that must be accounted for
This is a completeness/design-review list, not engineered dimensions or final component selection.

| Subsystem | Required parts/work to resolve |
|---|---|
| Chassis | Reviewed tubing and cut list, joints, brackets/gussets as justified, floor/foot support, motor plate, axle bearing mounts, seat mounts, battery tray and positive retention, guards. |
| Rear running gear | Axle, keyed hubs, two rated wheels/tires, bearings/hangers, spacers, keys, collars and end retention; verify shaft diameter, keyways, wheel pattern and track width. |
| Front running gear | Two rated wheels/tires and bearings, left/right spindles, kingpins/bushings, mounting brackets and retention hardware. |
| Steering | Wheel, shaft/hub, shaft supports/bearings, steering arm, two correctly sized tie rods/joints, locking hardware and stops; check full-travel interference and geometry. |
| Brakes | Compatible rotor/hub and caliper OR reviewed complete donor system; pedal/pivot/return, cable and anchors OR master cylinder/line, mounting and adjustment. Electrical motor cutoff does not replace friction braking. |
| Drive | Motor/controller, confirmed chain standard, correct motor/axle sprockets and mounts, chain/master link, chain adjustment, guarded rotating components. No gas clutch/torque converter assumed necessary. |
| Battery/charging | Exact approved battery configuration and manufacturer-matched charger, terminal covers, robust mounting, charge inlet; verify current capability, chemistry, maximum voltage and controller cutoffs. |
| Electrical controls | Compatible returning pedal, power/key circuit, accessible drive shutdown, correctly rated DC interruption hardware, main fuse near source, cables/lugs/connectors/strain relief. Validate brake-input behavior and throttle fault behavior with exact controller documentation. Kit switches/fuses are not an automatic system-wide signoff. |
| Driver interface | Fitted seat and mounts, pedal reach/stops and foot containment. Restraint/rollover configuration requires experienced review as a whole; do not add a belt independently to an unreviewed chassis. |
| Fabrication/testing | Welding wire/gas, cutting/drilling supplies, suitable fasteners and torque data, measuring tools, multimeter, safe supports, applicable riding/fabrication PPE. Owned equipment must be inventoried rather than assumed free/available. |

## Compatibility checks before committing to a BOM
1. Set actual intended use, desired speed/runtime and design load; proposed private flat paved test area is a working concept, not a user-confirmed location or street-use authorization.
2. Inspect and measure donor parts with the experienced helper before designing around them. Reject damage or components without a defensible application rating; worn tires/brakes change cost.
3. Select one chain family throughout; match sprocket bore/key/hub/offset and ratio. A shared nominal motor power does not make two kits mechanically compatible.
4. Check battery full-charge voltage against controller limits, continuous/peak current against controller demand, charger chemistry and polarity, and protection/cable/interruption ratings. Do not infer runtime from Ah alone.
5. Have the helper review structure, steering, braking, guards and mounting; electrical expertise may also be needed. Build staged tests around the final design rather than promising a top speed now.
