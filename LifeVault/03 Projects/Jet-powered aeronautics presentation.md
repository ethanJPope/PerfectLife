## Resources and artifacts
Revised script and original attempt are in the current conversation. Research checked 2026-09-29:
- [Whittle history and firsthand test account, IMechE](https://www.imeche.org/news/news-article/12-04-30/Marking_the_75th_anniversary_of_Whittle_s_first_jet_engine.aspx): rejection, patent lapse, 1937 runaway to about 8,000 rpm. Air-raid siren is a sound comparison, not a verified nickname.
- [Science Museum W.1 record](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co30296/whittle-w1-jet-propulsion-engine-1941): Britain's first jet airplane used Whittle's W.1.
- [NASA turbojet explanation](https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/k-12/VirtualAero/BottleRocket/airplane/turbth.html): compressor, combustion, turbine, nozzle, and thrust.
- [DLR von Ohain history](https://www.dlr.de/de/aktuelles/nachrichten/2011/20111209_das-jet-zeitalter-begann-in-goettingen-100-geburtstag-von-hans-von-ohain_2283/@@download/file): six-minute He 178 first flight, 27 August 1939; He 280 flew under jet power in 1941 and did not become operational.
- [Polish Aviation Museum emergency ejection](https://muzeumlotnictwa.pl/en/kalendarium/first-ejection/): icing and first emergency ejection in the He 280. Another regional museum conflicts on year/name; spoken draft omits those specifics.
