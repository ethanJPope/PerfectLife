# Sub-$650 battery search

Ethan reaffirmed a $1,200 total-kart budget and requested a battery under $650 while retaining the Kunray KR5V V2 72 V / 5 kW kit.

Best documented battery found under that ceiling is the HotPaxx 72 V 20 Ah metal-case EVE 40P pack at $582 shipped in the lower 48, with seller claims of 150 A continuous discharge and an included 5 A charger. A $624 HotPaxx 24 Ah option adds some runtime. MDPowerPacks lists a $499.99 20 Ah metal-case Samsung 50S/JK-BMS pack, but its public technical and warranty evidence is materially thinner.

The 20 Ah HotPaxx pack plus the $389.90 Walmart motor kit leaves $228.10 of the complete budget; the 24 Ah option leaves $186.10. No all-new high-quality chassis/running-gear/protection BOM has been verified within that remainder. At 85% usable energy, 20 Ah requires no more than about 1.63 kW average to last 45 minutes; 24 Ah requires no more than about 1.96 kW. The budget, motor choice and 45-minute target cannot all be treated as assured without substantial owned/donor components.
