# Thirty Ah budget battery search

Ethan likes the approximately $600 battery price but wants 30 Ah at comparable quality. The leading direct-buy candidate is Bomb Moto's $599 72 V/30 Ah battery with Samsung 21700 cells, ANT Bluetooth BMS, protective casing, adapters and 5 A charger. Bomb Moto recommends an 80 A tune and warrants 70 A; the design should therefore use 60 A for commissioning and 70 A as its supported operating ceiling unless Bomb Moto confirms otherwise in writing.

At an 85% usable-energy assumption, the 2.16 kWh nominal pack provides an estimated 55/44/37 minutes at 2/2.5/3 kW average input. The pack plus the Walmart Kunray kit totals $988.90 and leaves $211.10 of the $1,200 target before shipping/tax and remaining parts.

The candidate is not purchase-ready because its cell model, exact continuous rating, dimensions, weight, BMS/regen settings and transport documents are unpublished, and Bomb Moto's FAQ and warranty page conflict between three and six months of battery coverage. AMORGE's Samsung 50S 20S6P pack is a technically stronger quote alternative only if its delivered written quote including charger stays within $650.
