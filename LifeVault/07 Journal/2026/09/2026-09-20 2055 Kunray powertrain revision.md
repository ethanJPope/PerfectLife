# Kunray powertrain revision

Ethan asked to rebuild the go-kart power-system recommendation around the Walmart-listed Kunray KR5V V2 72 V / 5 kW motor kit with its 100 A Fardriver/Kunray NS18 controller. This is the current design direction and supersedes the QS138/Kelly and Blwny-battery paths; nothing was purchased.

Strict-evidence research supports a 20S lithium-ion battery at 72 V nominal and 84 V full charge, preferably 40–45 Ah and at least 100 A continuous. Current candidates are the $930 Chakra 72 V 40 Ah EVE-40P pack for value and the $1,599 ChiBatterySystems 72 V 45 Ah Pulsar for stronger documentation. The Chakra listing has unresolved charger-voltage and dimensional conflicts, so it is not purchase-ready. Research and calculations: [[05 Knowledge/Go-kart 72 V budget revision#Kunray KR5V power-system revision, 2026-09-20]].

Next design input needed: final rear-tire outside diameter. That determines the 420-chain sprocket ratio for the 30 mph maximum target.
